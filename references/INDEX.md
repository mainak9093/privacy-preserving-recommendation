# `references/` — the surveyed papers

PDFs for the works cited in [`../report/references.bib`](../report/references.bib).
**50 of 66 obtained.** Total ~46 MB.

Filenames match the BibTeX citation key (`grotto2023.pdf` <-> `\cite{grotto2023}`), except
the four downloaded by hand before this convention was adopted, noted below.

Acquisition was automated: DOI -> Semantic Scholar open-access lookup, then IACR ePrint
title search, then arXiv title search. **Every automatic download was title-verified**
against the BibTeX entry before being kept, so no file here is the wrong paper.

Where a paper is published at a paywalled venue (ACM DL, IEEE Xplore) the local copy is the
author's ePrint or arXiv version; **the citation in the survey is always the published
version**, which is the one DBLP verified.
