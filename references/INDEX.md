# Every work cited

All 67 entries in [`report/references.bib`](../report/references.bib), generated from that file, with a
link to each publisher or preprint page. The papers themselves are not redistributed here. For the two
papers the project builds on, and reading by topic, see [`README.md`](README.md).

## How the citations were verified

Bibliographic metadata was retrieved from [DBLP](https://dblp.org) rather than typed by hand. Two entries that
DBLP could not supply are built by hand and flagged at the entry in the `.bib` file.

Papers were acquired automatically, by DOI lookup, then ePrint and arXiv title search. **The first pass silently
downloaded 11 wrong papers.** Title similarity is not a sufficient check, because a superset title passes
trivially: "From Lattices to Tensor Cores: Accelerating Private Information Retrieval" contains every word of
Chor et al.'s title. An author check caught all 11: at least half the surnames in the entry must appear on the
first page of the PDF. Of the works cited, 40 were obtained, and
[`report/notes/evidence.md`](../report/notes/evidence.md) records what was read and how far each claim in the survey can be traced.
The survey makes no claim about a work we could not obtain that is not attributed to a source we did read.
