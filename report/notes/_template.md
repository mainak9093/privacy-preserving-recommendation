## 1. Problem
What goes wrong without this work. Who is the victim, what leaks.

## 2. Threat model
Parties, how many, corruption model (semi-honest / malicious), collusion bound, what is
assumed public. **Be exact** — this is the part most summaries fudge, and it is what the
viva will ask about.

## 3. Technique
The actual mechanism, in enough detail that you could sketch it at a whiteboard. Name the
primitives. If there is one central trick, say what it is and *why* it works.

## 4. Results
Concrete numbers with their setting: dataset, scale, hardware, network. A number without its
setting is not a result.

## 5. Stated limitations
What the authors themselves say they do not do. Quote the non-goals section if there is one.
**These are where projects find their gaps** — an author-acknowledged limitation is a far
safer target than one you think you spotted.
