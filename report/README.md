## Figures

`figures/` holds PDFs generated by `mingw32-make figures` from the raw rows in [`bench/results/`](../bench/results/). They are
never edited by hand and not committed, so run that target once before building the report on a fresh clone.

## Notes

[`notes/`](notes/) holds the reading notes behind the survey. [`evidence.md`](notes/evidence.md) is the working tracker that ties
each claim in the survey to what we actually read, and [`forward_citation_sweep.md`](notes/forward_citation_sweep.md) records the
check for counterexamples to its central claim. [`_synthesis.md`](notes/_synthesis.md) is a pre-reading hypothesis, kept for the
record and superseded by the survey itself.

## Conventions

- Notation is fixed in `preamble.tex`, and sections do not define their own symbols.
- `references.bib` comes from DBLP. Hand-built entries are flagged at the entry.
- Numbers carry their setting. "50 minutes" is not a result, and "50 minutes on three 192-core servers over a LAN" is.
